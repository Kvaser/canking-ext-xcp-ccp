Kvaser CanKing 7 DAQ Decoder extension
===============================================================================


New Features and Fixed Problems in version 1.1.0
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Requires Kvaser CanKing 7.6 or later.
- Commands are padded to 8 bytes, or to MAX_DLC on CAN FD when the A2L sets MAX_DLC_REQUIRED
- Dynamic DAQ lists are numbered from MIN_DAQ instead of 0
- Each signal's ECU_ADDRESS_EXTENSION is sent in WRITE_DAQ
- Fixed a signal sharing an ODT entry with part of a split signal being decoded from the wrong bytes


New Features and Fixed Problems in version 1.0.0
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Requires Kvaser CanKing 7.6 or later.
- Initial release.
