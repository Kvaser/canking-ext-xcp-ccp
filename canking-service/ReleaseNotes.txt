Kvaser CanKing 7 DAQ Decoder extension 1.1.0
===============================================================================

Requires Kvaser CanKing 7.6 or later.

New Features
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- The DAQ list prescaler set in the DAQ Configurator.
- MAX_DLC_REQUIRED: XCP commands are padded to 8 bytes, or to MAX_DLC on CAN FD. CCP commands are always 8 bytes.
- MIN_DAQ: dynamic DAQ lists are numbered from MIN_DAQ instead of 0.
- ECU_ADDRESS_EXTENSION: each signal's address extension is sent in WRITE_DAQ.

Bug Fixes
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- A signal sharing an ODT entry with part of a split signal was decoded from the wrong bytes.
